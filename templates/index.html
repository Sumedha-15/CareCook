🤖 How It Works

Three layers of AI protection between your ingredients and your plate

01
🌿

ML Safety Filter

A Random Forest model trained on 3,000+ food items analyses Sugar, Fat, Sodium, Carbohydrates, and Cholesterol for each ingredient. It outputs a safety score of 0 (unsafe), 1 (caution), or 2 (fully safe).

→
02
⚖️

Condition Thresholds

Each of the 8 medical conditions has strict per-nutrient limits. If you have multiple conditions, the strictest threshold for each nutrient is automatically applied. No ingredient that violates a limit reaches your recipe.

→
03
🍳

Llama-3 AI Chef

Only ML-cleared ingredients are passed to Llama-3 70B, which acts as your personal nutritionist-chef. It generates three dish suggestions, then a full recipe with cooking steps, portions, and a health explanation.
